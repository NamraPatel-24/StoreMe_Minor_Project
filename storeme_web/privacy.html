Privacy Policy for StoreMe

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

StoreMe ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles your data. StoreMe operates as a Peer-to-Peer (P2P) personal cloud server.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

Because of the P2P nature of StoreMe, we do not collect, store, or have access to any of your files, photos, videos, or documents. When you transfer files between devices, they are transmitted directly over End-to-End Encrypted (E2EE) WebRTC data channels. Your files never rest on our servers.

3. Data Used for Connection (Signaling)

To establish a direct connection between your devices, we use Google Firebase Realtime Database for "signaling". This means temporary routing data (like IP addresses and WebRTC ICE candidates) is briefly exchanged to connect your devices. This data is strictly used for connection establishment and is not permanently stored or used for tracking.

4. Permissions

The StoreMe Android app requires storage permissions strictly to allow you to serve your own files to your own connected devices. We do not access your storage for any other purpose.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date of this Privacy Policy.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the Google Play Store contact information.
